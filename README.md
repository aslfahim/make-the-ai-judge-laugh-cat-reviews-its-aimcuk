One funny single-line one-star review from a house cat about its human:
one star, because the human works from home now and follows the cat from
room to room like a stray — no privacy, not even at the litter box.

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes that one line to `/output/entry.txt`.

Entry for the [make.fun competition](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-cat-reviews-its-42a34990).
